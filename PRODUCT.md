# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is hiring managers and recruiters screening for senior (VP/Director-level) engineering leadership roles — skimming quickly for credibility and technical taste before deciding whether to reach out. A secondary audience is engineering peers and leaders who land here through networking and assess depth of technical judgment more slowly. Both audiences arrive with limited attention and compare this site, consciously or not, against every other engineer's portfolio they've seen that week.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Asim Khan, Engineering VP at Goldman Sachs, built to generate interview opportunities and recruiter outreach for senior engineering leadership roles. Success looks like a hiring manager leaving with a clear, credible sense of technical depth and taste within the first screen, and following through to LinkedIn, GitHub, or the resume download.

## Positioning

Proof of a hands-on builder at VP level. Most engineers at this seniority stop shipping code; this site itself — its craft, its motion, its restraint — is the evidence that Asim still does. The medium argues the claim.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, quietly confident. Voice favors understatement over hype — the code-flavored tagline (`while (alive) { learn(); build(); inspire(); }`) and cinematic scroll-reactive background carry the personality; copy itself stays plain and factual. Confidence is shown through execution quality, not adjectives.

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS-template portfolio: no cookie-cutter card grids, no gradient-text hero, no stock "available for hire" scaffolding. Not an overloaded resume-dump: no wall-of-text CV reformatted as a webpage with flat hierarchy. Motion and 3D depth exist to reinforce technical credibility for a hiring-manager audience, not to perform as an agency/creative-studio showcase.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell — technical claims (fintech scale, AI tooling fluency, systems depth) are backed by the site's own engineering (custom hooks, scroll-reactive video, 3D tilt), not by adjectives in the bio copy.
- Precision over spectacle — every animation and glass effect earns its place; restraint reads as seniority.
- Fast credibility — a hiring manager should form a clear impression within the first viewport, before scrolling.
- Practice what you preach — the code quality and craft of the site itself is part of the pitch, not just its content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Reduced-motion accommodations are required throughout (video background, idea-stream canvas, and 3D tilt/parallax effects already swap to static/poster-frame alternatives and must stay that way as new motion is added).
