## Overall Impression

Real design-system craft exists here (the glass token system, universal reduced-motion handling, resilient GitHub fetch) — this isn't a template. But the page over-delivers on spectacle and under-delivers on the one fact that actually closes the deal: a recruiter scanning the first viewport sees a typewriter cycling "Gamer / Foodie / Fitness Freak" before they see "Engineering VP, Goldman Sachs." The single biggest opportunity is subtraction — cut the concurrent-motion count and put the credibility signal where the audience is actually looking.

[P2] Contrast failure + mobile overflow risk.
Why it matters: Hero.tsx:19 typewriter color #0320ff on #18181B is ~2:1 contrast — "Fitness Freak" nearly invisible in the most-viewed viewport. Hero.tsx:191 tagline uses whitespace-nowrap on a 46-char string; will clip at ~320px width.

Suggested command: /impeccable audit

[P2] No focus states, silent errors.
Why it matters: no :focus-visible rule exists anywhere in index.css for glass buttons, nav, or cards — keyboard users get only a weak UA default over a blurred field. GitHub-fetch failure and the empty-repos path both render with zero user-facing message.

Suggested command: /impeccable harden
