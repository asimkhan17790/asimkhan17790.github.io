## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Does this look AI-generated? Yes — within ~2 seconds. Not the bland-template failure; the opposite — maximalist tech-cliché overload.

LLM assessment (A): Two redundant "I am a coder" motifs stacked — a laptop-code stock video AND Matrix binary rain (IdeaStream.tsx), the single most literal hacker/AI cliché on the web. The canvas is position:fixed behind text with no backing panel, so digits render through copy ("collaborati0ns" on the Contact heading). Eyebrow kicker on all six sections (and repeated on each Skills card). Projects is an uncurated GitHub dump (filter(r => !r.fork) only) padding the grid with java-learning, pythonLearning ("No description."), coding-practise-python — resume-dump content that signals junior.

Deterministic scan (B): detector exit 2 both runs.
- repeated-section-kickers ×6 — agrees with A: eyebrow before every h2.
- gradient-text ×1 — ScrollProgress gradient + hero .text-shimmer (background-clip:text).
- gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow ×1 on .glass-btn — 1px border + 22px shadow blur. Tension: A judges the glass system "earned" (real inset sheen, tint-mix, consistent); the detector still reads it as a tell. Both true — well-executed AND a recognizable pattern.
- overused-font — Inter at 90% of text. Identity-preserving (already shipping), low priority.
- design-system-color ×9 — mostly legit (video overlays, shadow tints); the 6 Hero values are the hobby-carousel palette.

Measured facts (B): .text-muted on body bg = 6.98:1 (passes AA) — but only on solid bg; muted text composited over the moving video was not measurable and is the real risk. Horizontal overflow at 390px = 0px (clean).

Reduced-motion conflict resolved by synthesizer: motion IS handled via framer-motion useReducedMotion() across 14 components (IdeaStream/VideoBackground/useTilt gate on it). B's "not honored" was a grep false-negative (searched literal prefers-reduced-motion/matchMedia). Real gap: only scroll-behavior:smooth (index.css:45) is ungated.

## Overall Impression

The bones are genuinely strong — a real physically-modeled glass system, a clean type scale, systematic reduced-motion gating. All of it is buried under two overlapping "coder" backgrounds and an uncurated repo wall. Biggest single opportunity: the first viewport sells a hobby, not the pitch.

## What's Working

1. A real glass system (.glass-btn, index.css) — inset sheen, color-mix tint, brand-tinted active state, translate-on-hover. Cohesive, not the template default.
2. Systematic reduced-motion handling — useReducedMotion() across 14 components: IdeaStream returns null, VideoBackground swaps to poster, typewriter collapses, useTilt disables.
3. Skills taxonomy + Experience timeline — the two most scannable, credibility-forward blocks. This is the register the whole site should live in.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Hero states a hobby, not the pitch. Hero.tsx:13-20,187 + profile.ts
- Why: The brief's success metric is "clear technical depth + taste within the first screen." Screen one delivers "Asim Khan → I am Foodie → while(alive){...} → 4 buttons" over binary rain. The positioning line (Engineering VP at Goldman Sachs, 12+ yrs, cloud-native data platforms) sits in profile.ts but only appears below the fold.
- Fix: Lead hero sub-headline with real positioning (mono kicker "Engineering VP · Goldman Sachs" + one proof line). Demote/cut hobby carousel. Code tagline becomes tertiary.
- Command: /impeccable clarify

[P1] Decorative layers wreck legibility + are the loudest slop signal. IdeaStream.tsx, VideoBackground.tsx
- Why: Binary digits render through headings/body; muted copy over moving video drops composited contrast below AA in patches.
- Fix: Cut binary rain entirely. Confine video to hero viewport, dissolve to solid --bg past the fold. Any kept texture behind opaque panels, never bare text.
- Command: /impeccable quieter

[P1] Projects is an uncurated repo dump that signals junior. useGithubRepos.ts:80, Projects.tsx
- Why: For a VP pitch, java-learning / pythonLearning "No description." / coding-practise-python is negative evidence that dilutes the signal and contradicts "taste."
- Fix: Curate — drive Projects from a hand-picked data/projects.ts, or gate the fetch (require description, non-"learning" allowlist/topic, min activity). Never show No description. cards.
- Command: /impeccable distill

[P2] Eyebrow-on-every-section + too much simultaneous motion. SectionHeading.tsx (detector: repeated-section-kickers ×6)
- Why: Systematized kicker is textbook AI scaffolding; combined with 6+ concurrent motion systems it violates the brand's own "understatement" principle.
- Fix: Break the cadence — vary section entrances, drop the kicker on most sections. Reduce concurrent motion.
- Command: /impeccable typeset + /impeccable quieter

[P3] A11y + redundancy polish. index.css (no focus rules, scroll-behavior:smooth ungated), Nav.tsx, Contact.tsx
- Why: Zero :focus-visible rules → keyboard users get low-contrast default ring over glass. scroll-behavior:smooth (index.css:45) not wrapped in @media (prefers-reduced-motion). Three theme toggles. Contact CTA doesn't ask for the interview.
- Fix: Add :focus-visible ring (2px --spark offset) to .glass-btn + links; aria-current on active nav; wrap smooth-scroll in reduced-motion guard; keep one toggle; rewrite CTA toward intent.
- Command: /impeccable audit then /impeccable clarify
